Privacy Policy

Eksoch Inventory Manager by Eksoch Analytics. Last updated: 24 November 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Eksoch Analytics does not run a server for this app and never receives your data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

What stays on your device

Your shop details, products, stock levels, purchases, sales, suppliers and customers are stored only inside this app on your device (in the browser's local storage). Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Backups and CSV files you download are saved where you choose, and you decide who sees them.

Notifications

If you turn on low-stock alerts, your browser shows notifications made by the app on your device. No push service or server is involved, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings or in your browser.

Your Pro code

If you enter a Pro code (for example from AppSumo), it is checked inside the app on your device and saved only there. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Removing your data

Open Settings → Delete all my data from this device, or uninstall the app. Because your data exists only on your device, we can't recover it for you, so keep a backup.

Contact

Questions? Contact Eksoch Analytics through eksochanalytics.gumroad.com.
